    How these numbers are made
    Neonatal death
    A live-born baby who died at age 0–27 completed days (WHO). A death on the programme's "day 28" (date of birth + 28) is counted as post-neonatal. Early neonatal = 0–6 days.
    NMR denominator
    Live births whose day-28 outcome is known (alive, or died). Babies not yet 28 days old, not reachable, or not followed up are left out of the rate and counted under "outcome known". With 94–97% known, the rate may be slightly overstated if missing babies are more likely to be alive.
    Stillbirth rate
    Stillbirths per 1,000 total births (live + still).
    95% CI
    Wilson interval for the proportion of known live births who died. Wide whiskers mean few births: read those rates with care.
    Sources
    2024: Sankalp Khargone REDCap project (one form per pregnancy, outcomes collected retrospectively, median about 5 months after birth). 2025–26: NHRP Sankalp REDCap project (birth notification, registration and a day-28 phone follow-up per baby). SNCU: DH Khargone admission register, May 2024 – Sep 2026.
    Where
    Block of residence as recorded. "Out of district" = mother not a usual resident of Khargone.
    Gestation
    Mostly from LMP. Values outside 22–44 weeks are treated as missing and flagged.
    SNCU linkage
    SNCU admissions were matched to cohort babies on family phone number + date of birth, or both parents' names + date of birth (within 2 days). 75% of admissions matched; match quality: 97% agree on sex, median weight difference 80 g.
    Privacy
    This page holds no names, phone numbers, villages or exact dates. Records are identified only by REDCap Record ID or SNCU admission ID, and staff by codes, so errors can be looked up in REDCap.
